User Manual

Everything you need to know to draw, organize and sync your notes in KeedKien (ขีดเขียน).

1Getting started

The first time you open the app, you'll be asked to choose a display language. It defaults to your phone's language when supported, or English otherwise — you can change it later at any time.

Next, sign in with your Google account. KeedKien only uses this to identify you and to request access to a private, app-only area of your Google Drive (called drive.appdata) used for backing up your notes — it cannot see or touch the rest of your Drive files.

KeedKien is built for stylus input only. Drawing with a finger is intentionally disabled — always use a pen/stylus on the canvas.

2Sketchbook & notes

The Sketchbook tab lists every note you've created, newest first. Tap any entry to reopen it exactly as you left it, or tap New drawing to start with a blank page.

  • Edit / Delete — swipe or use the menu on a note to rename or permanently remove it.
  • Folders — group related notes together. Create a folder, move notes into it, and filter the list by folder. Deleting a folder deletes every note inside it, so the app always asks you to confirm first.

3Drawing tools

The toolbar at the bottom of the drawing screen holds everything you need while keeping the canvas clear.

Pens & color

  • Pen types — Ballpoint, Fountain Pen, Pencil and Highlighter, each with its own feel.
  • Color & palettes — pick from Classic, Pastel & Highlighter, Earth Tone or Vibrant & Neon palettes, use a custom color, or reuse a recent one.
  • Pen size — adjust stroke thickness to match the tool.

Erasing

  • Stroke eraser — removes a whole stroke with one touch.
  • Point eraser — erases only where the pen actually passes, like a traditional eraser.

Shapes, text & extras

  • Shapes — line, arrow, rectangle, circle, triangle and star, with optional fill color.
  • Text box — add and edit typed text anywhere on the page.
  • Stickers and tape — decorative elements you can drop onto the page.
  • Lasso select — select a region to move, copy or delete.
  • Images — insert a photo, then drag to move, resize, rotate, lock or delete it.
  • Paper style — switch the background between blank, lined or grid.

Navigation

  • Undo / redo — step backward or forward through your changes.
  • Zoom in / out / reset — get precise or step back to see the whole page.
  • Quick presets — save your favorite tool + color + size combination for one-tap reuse.

4Documents & images

The Documents tab lists PDF files found on your device (search by name or filter by date), and the Images tab does the same for image files (every common format except GIF). Tap a file to open it in the drawing screen and start writing directly on it.

Multi-page PDFs render as one continuous, scrollable canvas — no page-flipping required.

5Pages

A note can hold multiple pages. Use the page tools to add a new page, duplicate the current one, delete a page, or open the page overview to jump straight to any page in the note.

6Tasks

The Tasks tab is a bullet-journal style checklist. Each task can be created in two ways:

  • Type text — a plain typed task.
  • Write with pen — hand-write the task directly with your stylus.

Give a task a due date and a reminder time, and optionally link it to a sketchbook note (or create a new one for it) so you can jump straight from the task to your related drawing. Filter the list by All, Today, Tomorrow, No date or Completed, and mark items done with a strikethrough.

7Saving & exporting

  • Save — stores your note. When exporting a drawing you're prompted to choose where the file is saved on your device.
  • Export / Share as JPG — turn your note into a JPG image you can share with any app.
  • Clear — wipes everything drawn on the current note. This always asks for confirmation, since it can't be undone once saved.
Leaving a note with unsaved drawings always shows a confirmation, so you never lose work by accident.

8Sync & backup

Your notes are stored on your device first. Whenever your phone has an internet connection, KeedKien automatically syncs everything to your private Google Drive app storage roughly every 5 minutes. If you're offline, syncing simply pauses until you're back online — nothing is lost.

You can also trigger a sync manually any time from Settings → Sync now. The button is disabled while offline and shows the current sync status (Syncing… / Synced / Offline).

9Settings

  • Profile — your name, email and current subscription status.
  • Sync — manual sync button and Google Drive storage usage.
  • Language — switch between English, ไทย, Bahasa Indonesia, 日本語 and 한국어.
  • Theme — Light or Dark.
  • Sign out — sign out of your Google account on this device.

10Subscription

KeedKien includes a 45-day free trial with full access. After the trial ends, continuing to use the app requires subscribing to the single yearly plan at ฿189/year, managed through Google Play. You can restore a previous purchase from the subscription screen if you reinstall the app or switch devices.

11Tips & troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens when I draw with my finger — this is expected. KeedKien only accepts stylus/pen input, by design.
  • Sync button is greyed out — your device is offline; reconnect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again.
  • I unchecked Drive permission during sign-in — sync needs the Drive app-data permission. Sign out and sign in again, making sure to allow "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive".
