Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

KeedKien (ขีดเขียน), package name com.wanwan.prom.note ("the app", "we", "us"), is a pen-only drawing and note-taking app for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how it is protected. We designed the app to be local-first: there is no backend server or database that stores your notes — your content lives on your device and, optionally, in your own Google Drive.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Google account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile — name, email address and profile picture — from Google, used only to identify your account inside the app and to display it in Settings.

1.2 Google Drive access (App Data folder only)

We request the drive.appdata scope, which grants access to a hidden, app-specific folder in your Drive — not your regular Drive files or folders, and not visible in the normal Google Drive interface. We use this folder exclusively to back up and sync:

  • Your notes and drawings
  • Your tasks
  • Your subscription status (start date and expiry)

This lets you restore your notes if you reinstall the app or switch devices. We never request or access your general Google Drive files.

1.3 Content you create

Your drawings, imported PDFs and images, notes, folders and tasks are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database, and synced to your Drive app-data folder as described above.

1.4 Subscription & billing

Subscriptions are purchased and processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, see or store your payment card details — Google handles all billing information under its own privacy policy.

1.5 Information we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers, ad tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or call/SMS data
  • No content of your general Google Drive (only the app-data folder described above)
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone

2. How your data is used

DataPurpose
Name, email, profile pictureIdentify your account and display your profile in the app
Notes, tasks, drawings (Drive app-data)Back up your content and sync it across your own devices
Subscription statusDetermine whether you have active access after the free trial

3. Where your data lives

Two places only: (1) an SQLite database on your device, and (2) the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive account. Neither we, nor any third party, operate a server that stores copies of your notes.

The only server component we operate is a small Cloudflare Worker used solely to complete Google's sign-in flow (exchanging and refreshing OAuth tokens) — it is stateless, keeps no database, and stores nothing about you or your content. Your Google refresh token is kept only on your own device.

4. Data retention & deletion

  • On-device data is retained until you delete a note/task yourself, or uninstall the app.
  • Drive app-data is retained until you delete the corresponding note in the app, or revoke the app's Drive access from your Google Account settings, which removes the app's ability to read or write that folder.
  • To fully delete your account data, sign out, revoke KeedKien's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and uninstall the app.

5. Permissions we request on your device

  • Storage / file access — to list and open PDF and image files you choose to draw on, and to save exported drawings where you pick.
  • Network state — to detect whether you're online before attempting a Drive sync.
  • Notifications — to show task reminders you set yourself.

6. Children's privacy

KeedKien is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your rights

You may access, export (as JPG) or delete your notes at any time within the app. You may revoke Google Drive access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. If you would like us to help with a data-related request, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to wanwansweetdreams@gmail.com.
